# Legal AI Safety, Anti-Hallucination & Provenance Standard

## Strict Boundaries
1. **Zero Hallucination**: AI never invents non-existent legal provisions or fake Supreme Court citations.
2. **Temporal Law Awareness**: Matches the offense date with the effective law version.
3. **Human Decisions Record**: Distinguishes between AI suggestions and official officer determinations (`HumanLegalDecision`).
