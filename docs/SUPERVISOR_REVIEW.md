# Supervisor Review & Command Oversight Standard (กก.1 บก.ปคบ.)

## 1. Review Workflow
$$\text{Investigator Submit} \longrightarrow \text{Snapshot Bound} \longrightarrow \text{Supervisor Brief} \longrightarrow \text{Comment / Direction} \longrightarrow \text{Return or Approve}$$

## 2. Strict Human Governance
- AI never approves, rejects, returns, or signs on behalf of commanders.
