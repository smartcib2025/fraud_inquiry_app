# Legal Source & Authority Control Specification

## Official Legal Authority Hierarchy
1. `OFFICIAL_LAW`: Primary legislation (e.g. Criminal Code, Computer Crime Act, Cosmetics Act).
2. `REGULATION`: Ministerial regulations and royal decrees.
3. `NOTIFICATION`: Public Health / FDA notifications.
4. `COURT_DECISION`: Supreme Court precedents.
5. `INTERNAL_REFERENCE`: Police investigation directives.
