# AI Architecture & Orchestrator Design (กก.1 บก.ปคบ.)

## Core Principles
1. **No AI conclusion without source references**: Every fact or claim generated by AI must cite a valid `evidence_id`, `statement_id`, or `case_event_id`.
2. **No AI inference becomes fact automatically**: All AI findings require explicit human officer review (`ACCEPTED`, `PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED`, `REJECTED`).
3. **No AI-created accusation**: AI is an investigative copilot and assistant, not a legal judge.
4. **Data Minimization & Provenance**: Evidence content is treated as untrusted data input, preventing prompt injection attacks.
