# Digital Evidence & Forensic Source Extraction Model

## Digital Evidence Sources
- **Mobile Extractions**: Cellebrite / Oxygen extraction dumps.
- **Bank Transaction Ledgers**: Structured financial feeds and slip receipts.
- **Social Media & Chat Logs**: LINE Official chats, Facebook Messenger exports, TikTok live captures.
- **CCTV & Video**: Surrounding ATM camera recordings and surveillance video files.
