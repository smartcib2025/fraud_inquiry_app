# Legal Analysis & Statutory Mapping (กก.1 บก.ปคบ.)

## 1. Legal Reasoning Framework
The investigation copilot maps evidentiary facts to specific legal elements without declaring ultimate guilt:
$$\text{FACT} \longrightarrow \text{EVIDENCE} \longrightarrow \text{STATEMENT} \longrightarrow \text{TIMELINE} \longrightarrow \text{LEGAL ELEMENT} \longrightarrow \text{GAP} \longrightarrow \text{ACTION}$$

## 2. Distinction of Truth & Evidentiary Support
- `SUPPORTED`: Facts and evidence corroborating statutory element exist.
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`: Key element lacks corroboration; requires follow-up.
- **Strict Rule**: AI never concludes "the suspect is guilty".
