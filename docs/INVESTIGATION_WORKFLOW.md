# Investigation Workflow Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
**กก.1 บก.ปคบ. (Consumer Protection Police Division 1)**

## 1. End-to-End Investigation Lifecycle
1. **Intake & Triage**: Citizen complaints registered, initial urgency assessed, and slip/document OCR processed.
2. **Case Creation & Team Assignment**: Assigned to Unit (กก.1 บก.ปคบ.), Lead Investigator assigned, security classification determined (`CONFIDENTIAL` default).
3. **Issue Definition**: Formal `InvestigationIssue` items created to determine facts to prove.
4. **Evidence Acquisition & Chain of Custody**: Physical and digital exhibits registered, SHA-256 hashes calculated, custody transfer logged.
5. **Witness & Suspect Interrogations**: Question sets generated from evidence discrepancies, statements recorded in Q&A format.
6. **Timeline & Alibi Auditing**: Chronological reconciliation between physical evidence, IP logins, and suspect alibis.
7. **Legal Element Substantiation**: Facts and evidence matched against statutory penal codes and special consumer protection acts.
8. **Document Drafting & Multi-level Approvals**: Official police reports compiled, reviewed by Superintendent/Commander, and approved.
9. **Prosecution Submission**: Case readiness verified (100%), evidence bundle exported to Public Prosecutor.
