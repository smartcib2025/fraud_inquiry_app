# Statement AI Safety & Ethics Standard

## Mandatory Rules
1. **No automatic interrogations**: AI suggests questions; human investigators choose to ask them.
2. **No answer distortion**: AI never alters verbatim statements or converts denials into admissions.
3. **No fabrication of facts**: Any gap in evidence is explicitly tagged as `[ข้อมูลยังไม่ครบ / ต้องตรวจสอบ]`.
4. **No automated guilt labels**: Contradictions are presented as investigation leads, never as automated accusations of deception.
