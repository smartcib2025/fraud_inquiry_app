# Human Review & Conversion Workflow

## Review Actions
- **`ACCEPTED`**: Investigator ratifies the finding as credible.
- **`PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED`**: Investigator accepts part of the finding with officer annotations.
- **`REJECTED`**: Investigator dismisses the finding.

## Conversions
Investigators can convert accepted findings into:
1. `TIMELINE_EVENT` (Added to official case timeline)
2. `CASE_TASK` (Assigned to investigating officer)
3. `INVESTIGATION_ISSUE` (Added to proof issues)
4. `EVIDENCE_GAP` (Added to missing evidence register)
